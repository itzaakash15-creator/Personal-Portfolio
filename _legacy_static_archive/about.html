02 // IDENTITY

WHO I AM

I am a creator who moved into digital marketing, and is now building toward technology and entrepreneurship.

I am currently in my first year studying Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Rathinam Technical Campus in Coimbatore, with my roots in Tuticorin. I didn’t come into the digital world through theory or textbooks; I started by picking up a camera, dissecting video timelines, and teaching myself the mechanics of how visual pacing and tone capture human attention.

Over the past few years, that obsession with creative craft expanded into scripting, content strategy, and digital marketing. I moved from solo video creation to client campaigns, personal branding for models and professionals, and commercial agency work at Digi Marketrix.

Today, I stand at the threshold between human storytelling and computational systems. I believe that engineering without creative communication goes unnoticed, and marketing without technical substance doesn't scale. I am actively combining both.
