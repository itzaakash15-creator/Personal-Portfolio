# AAKASH K — Personal Portfolio
> **"NOTHING CHANGES IF NOTHING CHANGES."**

A minimalistic, premium, editorial personal portfolio for **Aakash K** — AI & Data Science Student, Digital Marketer, Content Creator, and Personal Branding Strategist.

Built with the design restraint of **Apple / Linear / Vercel / high-end creative agencies**.

---

## ⚡ Design Philosophy
- **Readability First:** Clean typography, generous whitespace, and immediate clarity.
- **Minimalistic & Intentional:** No dashboard clutter, no excessive glow or neon, no generic statistic bars.
- **Editorial Focus:** Large media areas, strict 12-column alignment (1400px global container), and crisp narrative hierarchy.
- **The Core Story:**
  1. **Hero:** Professional portrait, bold headline, and clear identity.
  2. **01 / Introduction:** Evolution from content creation to digital presence and technology.
  3. **02 / Digi Marketrix:** Professional agency internship across Scripting, Production, and Editing.
  4. **03 / U6NICK:** Editorial model personal branding (~3,000 followers, verified performance).
  5. **04 / Life With Aakash:** Personal creator platform & motivational speaking (59K peak reel, 17 reels, 453 followers).
  6. **More to Explore:** Minimal gateways to Web Platforms (Jayashakthi), AI & Hardware (SIH/ESP32), and The Journey.
  7. **Contact:** Direct email and verified social channels.
