MINEGUARDIAN / MINECORE

Integrated sensor network and telemetry dashboard designed for underground miner safety, real-time hazardous gas detection (CH4, CO, O2 levels), and seismic activity alerting.

THE PROBLEM

Underground coal and metal mines suffer from hazardous gas pockets (methane, carbon monoxide) and unstable shafts with zero line-of-sight communication.

THE CONCEPT

A distributed mesh of ruggedized sensor nodes monitoring atmospheric ppm thresholds and streaming telemetry to a surface command dashboard with automated emergency sirens.

TECHNOLOGY & STATUS

Tech: ESP32, MQ-4/MQ-7 gas sensors, MQTT, Node-RED dashboard, Python.
Status: Hackathon prototype & telemetry simulation completed.
